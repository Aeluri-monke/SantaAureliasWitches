JanitorAI: clone the lorebook

No download needed on JanitorAI. You can clone the lorebook straight to your own account and attach it to a bot.

↗ open the lorebook on JanitorAI
  1. Open the lorebook with the button above (or click it from Nate's bot page).
  2. Click the clone button on the lorebook page.
  3. Choose to assign it to a character, and pick the bot you want it on.
  4. Go to My Characters and open that bot. The lorebook is attached.

how to import

Character card: in SillyTavern, open the character panel, click the import button, and pick the PNG.
Lorebook: open World Info, click import, pick the JSON, then attach it to Nate or to your chat.
